Governance
•  Group management sets group-wide strategy, capital allocation and the operating standards every region works to.
•  Each of the six regional heads holds full profit-and-loss accountability and reports directly to the chief executive.
•  A quarterly review aligns all nine leaders on regional plans.
Regional governance
•  The same four group functions deploy into all six regions without local variants.
•  Each function lead owns one global blueprint and a named counterpart in every region, shown by the dot in that cell.
•  Regional heads and function leads co-own delivery; anything crossing a region escalates to group management.
