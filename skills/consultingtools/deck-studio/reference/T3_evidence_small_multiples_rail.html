What the client base shows
•  The four largest clients - Fennmoor, Harlow, Solvane and Brightwell - hold 328 of the 504 MEUR shown, about 65%.
•  Four new logos - Kestrel, Palisade, Ironhall and Meadowcross - grew from near zero to 9-12 MEUR within two years.
•  Clients with four or more practices average 61 MEUR, more than three times the 16 MEUR average with three or fewer.
•  Corvex and Tarnholt fell a combined 27 MEUR as legacy programmes completed; the other ten clients grew or held flat.
Read for the reader: growth is broad-based, and new logos scale quickly once won.
