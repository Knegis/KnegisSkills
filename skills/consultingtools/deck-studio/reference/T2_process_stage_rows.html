1
Site selection
Month 0-2
Regional operations director scouts sites against catchment and footfall targets.
Sites are benchmarked against Lumen's twelve-point location scorecard.
Lease terms and competitor density are tested before an offer is made.
2
Build and fit-out
Month 2-7
One fit-out contractor runs every clinic to the same floor plan.
Clinical equipment is ordered from three approved suppliers only.
Fit-out is signed off against the same forty-point checklist every time.
3
Opening
Month 7-9
The local opening lead hires and trains staff four weeks before opening.
A soft launch runs two weeks ahead of the public opening date.
Opening readiness is reviewed jointly by clinical and operations leads.
4
Stabilisation
Month 9-12
The regional director reviews utilisation and staffing weekly for ninety days.
A clinic exits the playbook once utilisation passes 70% for four straight weeks.
An underperforming clinic gets a revised staffing or marketing plan, not a write-off.
The 9-12 month playbook rests on three pillars
Consistency
The same scorecard, floor plan and checklist in every market.
Speed
One contractor and three suppliers keep the build to twenty weeks.
Local judgement
Regional directors can adapt staffing and marketing, never the clinical standard.
