How to read the spread
The gap is definitional, not disagreement. The two highest figures include bundled hardware or public fleets. Strip those and every source lands between 4.4 and 4.7 BEUR.
Growth is the firmer number. All five sources sit between 7% and 11% a year; the spread reflects how fast small fleets are assumed to adopt, not the direction.
The target's addressable share is smaller. It sells to fleets of 20 to 500 vehicles in six countries, which the own build puts at 1.9 BEUR of the 4.6.
Caveats
Ostrum and Kestrel forecasts were published before the 2026 emissions rules; both may understate compliance-driven demand.
The own build has not been reviewed by the fleet association; a call is booked for week 41.
Read for the reader: 4.6 BEUR is a defensible base case because three unrelated methods reach it. The valuation should not rest on the 5.3 headline.
